# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 15, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Calculator app (com.calculator.azaz) for mobile devices that was created as a free, open-source application.

## Information Collection and Use
We highly value your privacy. This application **does not** collect, store, or share any personal information, user data, or device identifiers. 

All features within the app—including standard calculations, unit conversions, GST calculations, tip calculations, and calculation history—are processed and stored completely locally on your device. No data is transmitted to any external servers or third-party services.

## Third-Party Access
Since the app does not collect any data, no data is shared with third-party services. The app does not include any third-party analytics or advertising frameworks that track user behavior.

## Security
We are committed to ensuring that your usage remains private. Because no data is transmitted over the internet by this application, there is no risk of your personal data being intercepted or breached through the use of this app.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
